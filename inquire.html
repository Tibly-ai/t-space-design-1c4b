Phase & Framework

Commission Protocol

Our ultra-minimal signature relies on a rigid physical development sequence. We follow a strict four-stage process to maintain alignment on texture, light, and construction.

01 / CONCEPT
Spatial Exploration & Massing
Initial analysis of natural site conditions, solar paths, and raw material responses. We establish basic geometric forms and core light pathways.
Weeks 1 – 4
02 / DEVELOP
Materiality & Tactility
Selection of physical mediums—poured concrete, dark raw steel, bleached oak, and custom plaster. Development of physical mockups at 1:1 scale.
Weeks 5 – 12
03 / DOCUMENT
Precision Architectural Drafting
Execution of absolute detail packages. Every shadow gap, recessed frame, and structural interface is drafted down to the millimeter.
Weeks 13 – 20
04 / REALIZE
Supervision & Master Building
Direct oversight on-site with handpicked builders. Continuous adjustments to plaster depths and light boundaries during raw assembly.
Ongoing
